Methodology and privacy

Sources and updates

We use validated snapshots of statistical APIs and versioned research releases. The automated process runs weekly; providers may publish less frequently or revise history. Each indicator retains its source, unit, years and retrieval date. These are not real-time observations.

Government periods

Only complete calendar years within each cabinet period are used; transition years are excluded. The average is the arithmetic mean of available annual observations, not the median. First/last means the first/last available annual observation, not the value on inauguration or departure day. Consecutive cabinets remain separate.

Changes and gaps

A change requires two observations. Absolute changes in percentage indicators use percentage points. Calculations precede rounding. Missing values stay missing: no replacement with zero or interpolation.

Interpretation

Earlier policies, crises, international conditions and time lags affect outcomes. Timing does not establish causation. We do not calculate an overall government score. European aggregates follow each provider’s methodology.

Privacy

We use no accounts, analytics or trackers. Saved indicators remain in your browser. The hosting provider may retain standard access logs. Email correspondence is also subject to the respective email providers.
