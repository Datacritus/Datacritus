What does this indicator measure?

This series records annual inflation for food and non-alcoholic beverages. It concerns this spending category rather than the full household consumption basket.

Original provider definition

Annual average HICP price change for food and non-alcoholic drinks, Eurostat ECOICOP version 2 CP01. Change in the annual average compared with the previous annual average, not December-on-December or the price level. A component of overall consumer inflation, not an additional rate to sum. Consumer expenditure weights apply; individual household experiences may differ. Government-period comparisons show the mean of available annual inflation rates, not cumulative price growth.

How to read the figures

Households experience different pressures depending on what they buy and the share of their income spent on food.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.

Why can food inflation differ from headline inflation?

Headline inflation covers a wider consumption basket, including categories such as housing, transport and services. Food inflation tracks food and non-alcoholic drinks, whose prices can change at a different pace.
