What does this indicator measure?

This survey indicator records people reporting unmet needs for medical examination or treatment due to cost, distance or waiting. The population and denominator follow the linked source definition.

Original provider definition

Share of people aged 16+ reporting an unmet need for medical examination because of cost, distance or waiting lists. Self-reported EU-SILC measure; not the share of rejected medical appointments. Components below are related and must not be treated as independent evidence.

How to read the figures

Self-reported unmet need is distinct from hospital capacity or treatment quality. Survey definitions and breaks should be checked before comparing years.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.

Which population does this healthcare access indicator cover?

This published series covers people aged 16 and over reporting unmet medical needs due to cost, distance or waiting lists. It is a self-reported survey measure, not a count of rejected appointments.
