What does this indicator measure?

Annual inflation is the percentage change in the consumer price index from the previous year. It describes the rate of price change, not the overall price level.

Original provider definition

Inflation as measured by the consumer price index reflects the annual percentage change in the cost to the average consumer of acquiring a basket of goods and services that may be fixed or changed at specified intervals, such as yearly.

How to read the figures

Falling inflation usually means prices are rising more slowly. It does not mean that prices have returned to an earlier level.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.

Annual observations and source flags
