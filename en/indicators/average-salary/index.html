What does this indicator measure?

The indicator is an average annual salary adjusted to full-time employment. It is a gross, nominal measure, not monthly take-home pay or the median worker’s salary.

Original provider definition

Average annual salary per employee adjusted to full-time work, national accounts and Labour Force Survey methodology. Nominal gross euros; not median pay, take-home income or inflation-adjusted purchasing power.

How to read the figures

A rise in nominal salary does not establish an improvement in purchasing power. Compare wages with inflation and housing costs.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.

Can this figure tell me the monthly salary I would receive?

It cannot determine an individual salary. The series is an annual gross average adjusted to full-time work. Taxes, contributions, working hours and the number of salary payments affect monthly take-home pay.

Annual observations and source flags
