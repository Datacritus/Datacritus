What does this indicator measure?

AROPE combines income poverty, severe material and social deprivation, and very low household work intensity. A person meeting more than one condition is counted once.

Original provider definition

EU 2030 AROPE definition: at least one of income poverty, severe material and social deprivation, or very low household work intensity. Components overlap; do not add their rates. Not spliced with the older AROPE definition.

How to read the figures

Income poverty is relative to the national median income. Survey reference periods and definition changes matter when reading the trend.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.

Can the components of poverty and social exclusion be added together?

The components overlap because a person can meet more than one condition. AROPE counts each person once, so adding the component percentages would double-count some people.
