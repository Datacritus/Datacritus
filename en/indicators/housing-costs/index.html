What does this indicator measure?

The housing cost overburden rate measures the share of people living in households whose housing costs exceed 40% of disposable household income, under Eurostat’s definition.

Original provider definition

Share of people living in households where total housing costs, net of housing allowances, exceed 40% of total disposable household income, net of housing allowances. EU-SILC; all ages, both sexes, total poverty status.

How to read the figures

This is a measure of affordability relative to income, not an index of rents or house purchase prices.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.

Does this indicator measure rent increases?

It measures housing costs relative to disposable income. The ratio can change because housing expenses change, household income changes, or both. Rent and property price indices answer different questions.
