Greece statistics and indicators

Explore Greece’s jobs, prices, housing, health and public finances through sourced historical data. Each indicator includes definitions, annual observations and links to the original provider.

Jobs & earnings

  • Unemployment in GreeceThe unemployment rate measures people without work who are available for work and seeking it, as a share of the labour force. It is not the percentage of the entire population without a job.
  • Youth unemployment in GreeceYouth unemployment measures unemployed people aged 15–24 as a share of the labour force in that age group. Students outside the labour force are not included in its denominator.
  • Average salary in GreeceThe indicator is an average annual salary adjusted to full-time employment. It is a gross, nominal measure, not monthly take-home pay or the median worker’s salary.

Living standards & housing

  • Inflation in GreeceAnnual inflation is the percentage change in the consumer price index from the previous year. It describes the rate of price change, not the overall price level.
  • Food inflation in GreeceThis series records annual inflation for food and non-alcoholic beverages. It concerns this spending category rather than the full household consumption basket.
  • Housing cost burden in GreeceThe housing cost overburden rate measures the share of people living in households whose housing costs exceed 40% of disposable household income, under Eurostat’s definition.
  • Poverty and social exclusion in GreeceAROPE combines income poverty, severe material and social deprivation, and very low household work intensity. A person meeting more than one condition is counted once.

Health & care

  • Life expectancy in GreeceLife expectancy at birth estimates how long a newborn would live if the mortality rates observed in that year continued throughout life.
  • Unmet healthcare needs in GreeceThis survey indicator records people reporting unmet needs for medical examination or treatment due to cost, distance or waiting. The population and denominator follow the linked source definition.

Public finances

  • Government debt in GreeceThis indicator expresses general government debt relative to gross domestic product. Both the debt stock and the size of the economy affect the ratio.
