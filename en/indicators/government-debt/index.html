What does this indicator measure?

This indicator expresses general government debt relative to gross domestic product. Both the debt stock and the size of the economy affect the ratio.

Original provider definition

Consolidated gross debt of general government (S13), at nominal value, as a percentage of GDP. Maastricht definition; distinct from central government debt.

How to read the figures

A lower debt-to-GDP ratio can occur while debt in euros rises. Read the denominator and economic growth alongside the debt figures.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.
