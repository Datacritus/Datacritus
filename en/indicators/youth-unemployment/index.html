What does this indicator measure?

Youth unemployment measures unemployed people aged 15–24 as a share of the labour force in that age group. Students outside the labour force are not included in its denominator.

Original provider definition

Youth unemployment refers to the share of the labor force ages 15-24 without work but available for and seeking employment.

How to read the figures

This is not the share of all young people who are unemployed. Education participation affects the size of the youth labour force.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.
