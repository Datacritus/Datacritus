What does this indicator measure?

The unemployment rate measures people without work who are available for work and seeking it, as a share of the labour force. It is not the percentage of the entire population without a job.

Original provider definition

Unemployment refers to the share of the labor force that is without work but available for and seeking employment.

How to read the figures

Lower unemployment can reflect changes in employment and participation. Read it alongside employment and wages.

Annual data describe published observation years. Publication dates can lag behind the current year, and providers may revise historical values.

Changes during a government’s term do not establish that its policies caused them.

Does a fall in unemployment mean more people have jobs?

It can, but unemployment also changes when people enter or leave the labour force. Check employment and labour participation alongside the unemployment rate.
