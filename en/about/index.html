About Datacritus

Purpose and initiative

Datacritus is a private initiative. It makes public data about Greece easier to explore and supports descriptive comparisons of government periods.

Independence

This is an independent project, without affiliation or endorsement from the Greek government, a political party or its data providers.

Funding and transparency

Datacritus is self-funded.
